Our Story

Started in Iba.
Still Here.

Tinu's Kitchen Bistro started at Ipaye Bus Stop, Iba — a cloud kitchen run by someone who genuinely loves to cook. Not a franchise. Not an investor's idea. Just Tinu, good ingredients, and a belief that the people of Iba deserved better food.

"Every plate I serve carries my love for cooking, my passion and the flavours that made me who I am as a chef."

Tinu, Founder

Today it's Jollof, pasta, sandwiches, milkshakes, catering and private dining. The kitchen has grown. The food is still made the same way it always was.
